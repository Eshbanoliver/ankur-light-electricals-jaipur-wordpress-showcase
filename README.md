# Ankur Light and Electricals - LED Lighting & Electrical Showroom Website


Custom WordPress website developed for Ankur Light and Electricals, a trusted lighting showroom and electrical solutions provider in Jaipur, Rajasthan (2025 client project).

## Project Overview
- **Client Industry**: Lighting & Electrical Retail (LED Lights, Decorative Fixtures, Flood Lights, Rope Lights, Street Lights, Event Lighting)
- **My Role**: Full custom WordPress development, theme customization, product showcases, performance optimization
- **Timeline**: 4-6 weeks
- **Team Size**: Solo developer
- **Live Website**: [https://ankurlight.in/](https://ankurlight.in/)  
  *(Confidential client project – visuals & case study only; full demo/login available on request after NDA)*

## Key Features Implemented
- Hero section with strong taglines ("We Proudly Deliver Brilliant Lighting Solutions", "Trusted by Electricians, Builders & Families", "Brightening Every Occasion With Elegance")
- Product/services showcase: LED Flood Lights, LED Rope Lights, LED Lamps, Fancy/Decorative Lights, LED Street Lights, Event & Custom Lighting Solutions
- About section highlighting 30+ years experience (since 1992), authorized Ledvance (Osram) distributor, genuine products, expert guidance, fast support, and flexible solutions
- Stats counters: 30+ Years Experience, 95% Repeat Clients, 90% Genuine Brands
- Testimonials carousel with real customer feedback on quality products, knowledgeable staff, polite service, easy installation, warranties, and overall experience
- Responsive design optimized for product visuals (mobile-first)
- SEO-friendly for local Jaipur lighting showroom searches

## Technical Stack
- WordPress v6.7+
- PHP 8.2+
- Elementor Pro (or similar builder) + custom CSS for bright, modern lighting styling and product grids
- JavaScript for testimonials slider, smooth interactions
- Plugins: Elementor, contact forms (WPForms or similar), Yoast SEO, image optimization/cache
- Performance: WebP images, lazy loading for high-res lighting photos

## Challenges & Solutions
- Challenge: Showcasing diverse lighting products attractively → Solution: Clean product grids/cards with visuals and trust badges (Osram authorized)
- Challenge: Building long-term trust (30+ years) → Solution: Stats on repeat clients/genuine brands + detailed testimonials
- Challenge: Local retail visibility → Solution: Jaipur-specific address + SEO for lighting/electrical searches


## What I Learned
- Customizing WordPress for retail lighting/electrical showrooms with visual product focus
- Using stats (95% repeat clients, 90% genuine) and testimonials to convey reliability
- Optimizing for local searches (Jaipur LED lights, decorative lighting showroom)
